# 🚗 Classic Models Sales Dashboard (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Classic Models Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard built using the Classic Models dataset. It provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance across countries, product lines, products, employees, and time periods. The dashboard enables users to explore business performance through interactive slicers, KPI cards, and dynamic charts.

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing countries and sales representatives.
- Evaluate monthly sales trends.
- Analyze product line and product performance.

## 📈 Business Insights

- 🇺🇸 USA generates the highest sales revenue, making it the strongest market.
- 📈 Sales peak in **November**, indicating strong seasonal demand.
- 🚗 **Vintage Cars** contribute the largest share (31%) of product line revenue.
- 👨‍💼 **Gerard Hernandez** is the highest-performing sales representative.
- 🏎️ **1992 Ferrari 360 Spider Red** is the top-selling product.
- 🌍 Sales are concentrated in a few major countries, highlighting opportunities for market expansion.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase marketing investment in high-performing markets like the USA and France.
- Prepare inventory before November to meet seasonal demand.
- Focus on expanding lower-performing markets through targeted campaigns.
- Analyze strategies used by top-performing sales representatives and replicate best practices.
- Continue investing in high-performing product lines while improving weaker categories.
